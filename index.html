AI Researcher · Independent Research

Developing Hybrid-DDQN solvers for VRPTW under strict independent cold-starts (cleared archive directory starting from build_greedy). On Homberger-200, both ALNS-Base and Hybrid-DDQN reach identical vehicle count floors; Hybrid-DDQN demonstrates superior consistency (0–20% NV degradation vs. 30–70% for ALNS) and 1.75% to 4.07% travel distance (TD) minimization at matched vehicle counts. On Homberger-400, Hybrid-DDQN achieves statistically significant vehicle count reductions on c2_4_1 (Mean: 12.20 vs. 13.00, Wilcoxon p = 0.0078) and r2_4_1 (Mean: 8.10 vs. 8.80, Wilcoxon p = 0.0156).
